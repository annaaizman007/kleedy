Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

In short:

  • Kleedy collects no personal data. No account, no servers, no ads, no tracking or analytics.
  • All your progress is stored only on your device. Delete the app and it’s gone.
  • Purchases go through Apple. We never receive any payment details.

Who we are

Kleedy is a piano-learning app for iPad and iPhone, developed and published by Anna Aizman, an independent developer based in Israel (“we” in this policy). You can reach us any time at kleedy.piano@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not send any information about its users to us or to anyone else: no name, no email address, no location, no device identifiers and no usage data. Kleedy contains no third-party advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting components, and there is no server of ours that the app connects to.

If you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with crash reports and aggregated, anonymous statistics. Apple manages this, and you can turn it off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

What is stored on your device

So the course remembers where you are, the app stores the following on your device only:

  • course progress, scores, stars and personal bests;
  • practice history, streaks, badges, and sight-reading and technique results;
  • settings — language, practice plan and reminders;
  • songs and MIDI or MusicXML files you add yourself. These files also appear in the Kleedy folder in the Files app.

This data never leaves your device. If your device is backed up to iCloud or a computer, it may be included in that backup according to Apple’s backup settings — we have no access to it.

Your piano and Bluetooth

The app asks for Bluetooth permission only to connect to a piano or keyboard via Bluetooth MIDI. The notes you play (MIDI signals) are processed on the device as you play, to give you feedback. They are not stored outside the app and are not sent anywhere.

Microphone

The current version of Kleedy does not use the microphone. We are planning a microphone mode for acoustic pianos. When it arrives, the app will ask for permission first, and audio will be analysed on the device only, in real time — never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. You will always be able to revoke the permission in your device settings.

Notifications

If you turn on practice reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device for the days and time you choose. No server sends them and we know nothing about them. You can turn them off in Kleedy’s settings or in your device settings.

Purchases and subscriptions

Kleedy Premium is purchased through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles the payment; we do not receive your name, email or payment details. The app checks with Apple, on the device, whether Premium is active. Apple’s Privacy Policy applies to that information.

When the app uses the internet

Kleedy works fully offline. It only goes online when you ask it to:

  • Buying and restoring purchases — through Apple, as described above.
  • Searching for chords online — if, while adding your own song, you choose to look up its chords on the web, the app opens a built-in browser with a DuckDuckGo search for the song title. The websites you open receive what any browser sends (such as your IP address), and their own privacy policies apply. We receive nothing from this.
  • Sharing your progress report — the PDF leaves your device only when you tap Share, and only to the place you pick.

Children

Kleedy is suitable for children. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app has no ads; purchases happen only through Apple’s system (which parents can limit with Screen Time or Ask to Buy in Family Sharing); and the only way to reach the internet from inside the app is the chord search described above. Parents can restrict web browsing with Screen Time.

Deleting your data

All of Kleedy’s data lives on your device, so deleting the app deletes it. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing to request — but you are always welcome to write to us.

Your rights

Under privacy laws (including the GDPR in Europe and Israel’s Protection of Privacy Law) you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data about you. Because we do not collect personal data, we hold none. If you email us, we use your address only to reply, and we keep the correspondence no longer than needed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes — for example adding the microphone mode — will also be mentioned in the app’s update notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to us: kleedy.piano@gmail.com
