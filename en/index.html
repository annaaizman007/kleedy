What the big apps don’t do

Most apps only say “right” or “wrong” and move you along. Kleedy works like a good private lesson: it understands what happened and shapes the next lesson around it.

🔍

Not just “wrong” — but why

After every piece you get a bar-by-bar error map in plain words: wrong note, came in early or late, skipped a note. One tap loops the weak bars slowly until they settle.

🎯

A daily workout built from your weak spots

5, 10 or 20 minutes, rebuilt every day from what’s still hard: the scale that isn’t flowing, the right reading level, the tricky bars from yesterday’s piece. Each part says why it’s there.

🎓

Move on when you’ve really got it

The course is built in stages, the way a teacher would. You pass a stage when the skill is truly there — not when you tap “Next”. And whatever starts to fade comes back just in time.

🎼

Read music, don’t just chase bars

Falling notes come with a real staff, so your eyes learn to read. A sight-reading test with 40 adaptive levels that keeps pace with you, plus ear and rhythm drills.

🎹

Technique, conservatory-style

Scales in all 12 keys, a tempo ladder that climbs step by step, and an evenness check that shows which finger is late. Stronger hands, no guessing.

🌟

52 pieces, three levels each

Classical pieces and traditional melodies in easy, medium and full arrangements. Stars and personal bests for every piece, practice streaks with a weekly rest day, badges, a monthly challenge and a PDF progress report to share with family or a teacher.

Works with your digital piano

Connect a digital piano or keyboard and Kleedy knows exactly which key you pressed and when. That’s how it can judge timing and evenness, not just whether the note was right.

  • USB: a cable from the USB TO HOST port on the back of the piano to your iPad or iPhone (straight into USB‑C, or through an adapter for Lightning).
  • Bluetooth MIDI: connect from the app’s settings, no cables.
  • No piano yet? Start on the on-screen keys — every exercise works that way too.
  • Acoustic piano: a microphone mode is on its way. Sound will be analysed on the device only — never recorded, never sent anywhere.
