Support & FAQ

Something not working, or have an idea? Answers to common questions are below. Didn’t find yours? Write to us.

Your piano

How do I connect a digital piano with a USB cable?
  • The square end of the cable goes into the port marked USB TO HOST on the back of the piano; the other end goes into your iPad or iPhone.
  • For a device with a USB‑C port you need a USB‑C to USB‑B cable. For a device with a Lightning port you need Apple’s Lightning to USB Camera Adapter in between.
  • Switch the piano on and open Settings in Kleedy (the gear icon). Your piano should appear in the list.
  • In the “Test” card, play one note — if the connection works, its name appears on screen.
How do I connect over Bluetooth?

If your piano has Bluetooth MIDI, open Settings in Kleedy and tap “Connect a Bluetooth MIDI instrument”, then pick your piano from the list. You don’t need to pair it in your device’s Bluetooth settings first. The first time, the app will ask for Bluetooth permission.

It’s connected, but the app doesn’t hear the piano
  • Make sure the piano is on and the cable is in the USB TO HOST port (not a port for USB sticks).
  • Some pianos have a menu setting called USB MIDI or MIDI OUT that needs to be turned on.
  • Unplug, plug back in, and tap “Refresh” in Kleedy’s settings.
  • Some cables only charge and don’t carry data — try a different cable.
I don’t have a digital piano. Can I still use Kleedy?

Yes. You can play on the on-screen keys, and every exercise works that way too. For acoustic pianos we’re preparing a microphone mode — sound will be analysed on the device only, never recorded or sent anywhere.

Purchases and subscription

How do I restore a purchase?

Make sure the device is signed in with the Apple ID you bought with, then tap “Restore Purchases” in the app. Premium comes back in a moment. Restoring needs an internet connection.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Kleedy → Cancel Subscription. To avoid another charge, cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. After cancelling, Premium stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I ask for a refund?

Payments go through Apple, so refunds do too: go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID and choose the purchase.

Using the app

How do I change the language?

In Kleedy’s settings (the gear icon) choose English, עברית or Русский. The whole interface switches instantly, including the text direction.

In Hebrew the interface currently addresses the user in the feminine form. A choice of form of address is coming in an upcoming update.

Do I need the internet?

No. All lessons, pieces and exercises are inside the app and work offline. You only need the internet to buy or restore a purchase, and for the online chord search if you choose to use it.

Where is my progress saved? What about a new device?

Your progress is stored only on your device — there’s no account and no server. To move it to a new device, restore the new device from an iCloud or computer backup, or use Apple’s Quick Start device-to-device transfer. Progress does not sync between an iPad and an iPhone — each device keeps its own.

How do I share a progress report?

On the Progress screen open “Achievements and report”, choose 7 or 30 days and tap Share. The report is created as a PDF you can send to a teacher or parents, or save.

How do I turn practice reminders on or off?

In Kleedy’s settings choose the days, the time and how many minutes. If reminders don’t arrive, check that notifications for Kleedy are allowed in your device’s Settings → Notifications.
